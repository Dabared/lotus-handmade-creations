Delivery & Returns

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Ordering

You can request an order through WhatsApp or our order form. An order is confirmed once we have agreed the item, colour, price, delivery charge and expected delivery date with you.

Delivery

  • We deliver island-wide within Sri Lanka.
  • Delivery charges depend on your location and the size of your order, and are confirmed with you before your order is finalised.
  • In-stock items are usually dispatched after your order and payment are confirmed.
  • Made-to-order and custom items are handmade after you confirm, so we'll give you an estimated completion date when you order.
  • Please make sure your delivery address and phone number are correct so the courier can reach you.

Returns & exchanges

  • If your item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, please contact us within 3 days of receiving it, with photos, and we'll make it right with a repair, replacement or refund.
  • Because every piece is handmade, small variations in colour, size and stitching are natural and are not considered defects.
  • Custom and personalised items (for example, items with a name or a special colour combination) are made specially for you, so they cannot be returned or exchanged unless they are faulty.
  • Items should be unused and in their original condition for any return.

Caring for your handmade item

We recommend gentle hand washing in cool water and drying flat in the shade. Specific care notes are listed on each product page.

Safety note

Our toys are handmade with care. For babies and very young children, please supervise play and check items regularly for wear, especially eyes, buttons and small decorations.

Questions?

Please contact us — we're always happy to help.
