Custom Handmade Orders

Have something special in mind? We'd love to make it for you — in your colours, your size and with your personal touch.

What we can make

  • Teddy bears and dolls in your choice of colours, outfits and accessories
  • Crochet animals and characters of your own design
  • Baby blankets, booties and newborn gift sets with a name embroidered
  • Bags, purses, hats and accessories in matching colour themes
  • Table doilies, napkin rings and coasters to match your home
  • Car mirror charms and tissue box covers
  • Party favours and return gifts in small batches

How it works

  1. Send us your idea, colours and any reference photos
  2. We confirm the design, price and expected completion date
  3. Once you confirm, we start handcrafting your piece
  4. We share a photo before delivering it to your door

Please tell us

  • What you'd like made and the approximate size
  • Preferred colours (or a theme)
  • Any name or personalisation
  • The date you need it by
  • Your delivery city

Because every custom piece is made by hand, timelines depend on size and detail. We'll always confirm the timeline with you before starting.
